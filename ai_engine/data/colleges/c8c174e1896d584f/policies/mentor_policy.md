
# Mentor Assignment Policy

## Mentor Assignment
Each student should have an assigned mentor based on their enrolled course.

## Mentor Responsibilities
- Guide students with academic difficulties.
- Help students understand course requirements.
- Support students who have attendance shortages.
- Provide guidance for assessment preparation.

## Mentor Availability
Mentor availability is measured in hours per week.

## Mentor Lookup
Students should be matched with mentors using their course ID.
