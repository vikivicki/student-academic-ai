
# Attendance Policy

## Minimum Attendance Requirement
Students must maintain at least 75% attendance in each course.

## Shortage of Attendance
- Students with attendance below 75% are considered short of attendance.
- Students should contact their assigned mentor for guidance.

## Attendance Calculation
Attendance Percentage = (Classes Attended / Total Classes) * 100

## Eligibility
Students who meet or exceed 75% attendance satisfy the attendance requirement.
