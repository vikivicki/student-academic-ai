
# Grading Policy

## Grade Calculation
The percentage is calculated as:

Percentage = (Marks Obtained / Total Marks) * 100

## Grade Classification
- 90% to 100%: A+
- 80% to below 90%: A
- 70% to below 80%: B
- 60% to below 70%: C
- 40% to below 60%: D
- Below 40%: F

## Passing Status
Students must achieve at least 40% to pass.

## Rounding
Percentages should be rounded to two decimal places.
