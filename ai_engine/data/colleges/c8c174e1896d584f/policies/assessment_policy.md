
# Assessment Policy

## Passing Requirement
Students must score at least 40% in an assessment to pass.

## Marks Calculation
Percentage = (Marks Obtained / Total Marks) * 100

## Assessment Status
- 40% or above: Pass
- Below 40%: Fail

## Final Marks
The final assessment percentage is calculated using the total marks obtained divided by the total maximum marks, multiplied by 100.
