# Grading Policy

## Percentage
Percentage = (Marks Obtained / Total Marks) * 100.

## Grade Bands
- 90% to 100%: A+
- 80% to below 90%: A
- 70% to below 80%: B
- 60% to below 70%: C
- 40% to below 60%: D
- Below 40%: F

## Pass Requirement
A percentage of at least 40% is a pass.

## Rounding
Display percentages to two decimal places. Determine pass/fail and grade using the unrounded percentage.
