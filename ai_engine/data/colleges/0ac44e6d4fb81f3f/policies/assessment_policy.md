# Assessment Policy

## Passing Requirement
A student must score at least 40% in each required assessment to pass that assessment.

## Assessment Calculation
Assessment percentage = (Marks Obtained / Total Marks) * 100.

A score of 40% or above is Pass. A score below 40% is Fail.

## Date Handling
For evaluation using the project reference date, include assessment records dated on or before 15 January 2025. Do not use records after the evaluation date.

## Validation
Marks obtained and total marks must be numeric. Total marks must be greater than zero, and marks obtained must be between zero and total marks.
