# Attendance Policy

## Minimum Attendance Requirement
Students must maintain at least 75% attendance in each enrolled course.

## Attendance Calculation
Attendance percentage = (Classes Attended / Total Classes) * 100.

A student meets the attendance requirement for a course when the unrounded attendance percentage is at least 75%.

## Shortage
A student below 75% attendance in any enrolled course has an attendance shortage and should contact the assigned course mentor.

## Date Handling
For evaluation using the project reference date, include attendance records dated on or before 15 January 2025. Do not use records after the evaluation date.
