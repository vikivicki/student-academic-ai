# Mentor Policy

## Mentor Assignment
Each active student should be assigned to the mentor associated with the student's enrolled course.

## Matching Rule
Match students to mentors using course_id. Do not assign a mentor from a different course.

## Mentor Responsibilities
Mentors support students with:
- Academic difficulties and course requirements
- Attendance shortages
- Assessment preparation
- Guidance on course progress

## Availability
The available hours per week for each mentor are recorded in mentors.csv. Report the recorded value and do not infer additional availability.
